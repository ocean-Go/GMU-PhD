核心研究问题

How do policy shocks in institutionally unstable environments affect firm survival, resource allocation, and supply chain restructuring?

  • Firm Survival：贸易限制是否影响出口企业存活率？
  • Resource Allocation：政策不确定性是否导致资源错配？
  • Supply Chain：企业如何重构供应链？

Research Idea 进化轨迹

v0 · 经验观察

看到政策不稳定影响企业经营

像业务经验，不像学术问题。→ 抽象为可研究变量。

v1 · 研究方向

Trade policy × Institutional instability × Firm behavior

从经验场景变成研究领域；仍需明确结果变量。

v2 · 核心研究问题

Policy shocks → Firm survival / Misallocation / Supply chain

已有因果链与三个子问题；需补数据与识别策略。

v3 · 可执行研究设计

企业层面数据 + 政策冲击分析

Potential data: firm-level trade data, customs records, country-specific policy changes (Latin America)。→ 为每个子问题匹配数据源与方法。

首封邮件模板

Subject: Prospective PhD Applicant – Trade and Institutions Research Interest

Dear Professor Reinert, I hope this message finds you well. My name is Hui Ding, and I am preparing to apply for the Public Policy PhD program at George Mason University for Fall 2027. Over the past decade, I have worked in international trade and supply chain management, with a focus on Latin American markets. This experience has led me to develop a research interest in how policy instability shapes firm behavior. Specifically, I am interested in how policy shocks in institutionally unstable environments affect firm survival, resource allocation, and supply chain restructuring. I would be grateful if I could share a brief one-page research idea with you to get your thoughts on its potential fit. Thank you very much for your time. Best regards, Hui Ding
