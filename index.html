Research Idea

How do policy shocks in institutionally unstable environments affect firm survival, resource allocation, and supply chain restructuring?

  • Firm Survival：贸易限制是否影响出口企业存活率？
  • Resource Allocation：政策不确定性是否导致资源错配？
  • Supply Chain Adjustment：企业如何重构供应链以应对制度不稳定？

第一封教授邮件策略

  • 能不能直接发？ 可以。美国 PhD 申请中，这是常见且合理的 academic inquiry。
  • 什么时候发？ 周二至周四，美国东部上午 8:30–10:30；西班牙傍晚发送较合适。
  • 先发给谁？ 第一轮只发 2–3 位，优先 Reinert + 1 位 backup。
  • 期待结果？ 不是让教授承诺录取，而是建立弱连接、测试匹配度、获得反馈。
  • Follow up？ 7 天后只跟进一次；无回复就放下，不反复追。

推荐邮件标题与正文

Subject: Prospective PhD Applicant – Trade and Institutions Research Interest

Dear Professor Reinert, I hope this message finds you well. My name is Ding Hui, and I am preparing to apply for the Public Policy PhD program at George Mason University for Fall 2027. Over the past decade, I have worked in international trade and supply chain management, with a focus on Latin American markets. This experience has led me to develop a research interest in how policy instability shapes firm behavior. Specifically, I am interested in how policy shocks in institutionally unstable environments affect firm survival, resource allocation, and supply chain restructuring. I would be grateful if I could share a brief one-page research idea with you to get your thoughts on its potential fit. Thank you very much for your time. Best regards, Ding Hui

风险控制

风险表现应对
研究问题太像业务经验像故事，不像 PhD 研究。使用 policy shocks、firm survival、misallocation 等学术抓手。
非传统背景被低估缺论文、缺标准学术路径。强调现实问题识别能力、企业层面观察、政策意义。
教授邮件被忽略无回复是常态。短邮件、轻请求、一页研究想法、7 天后仅 follow up 一次。
材料分散SOP、Research Statement、邮件说法不一致。所有材料统一围绕一句核心问题展开。
